Kanzi is a toolchain for 3D automotive HMIs, with an authoring tool and an embedded runtime. We use it where your program has standardized on it, or where its workflow fits your team.

## Where Kanzi fits

- **Digital clusters and cockpit displays** with 3D content.
- **Multi-display cockpits** that share data and design language.
- **Designer-to-developer workflows** around a dedicated authoring tool.
- **Variants and brand themes** across vehicle lines.

## What we bring

- **Implementation from design.** UI and UX concepts turned into running, production-grade screens.
- **Custom components and integration.** Vehicle signals, middleware and platform services connected to the UI.
- **Performance.** GPU and CPU profiling and tuning on target hardware, with [3D assets](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/cad-to-realtime-pipeline/) prepared for the runtime.
- **Migration.** Moving a UI between Kanzi, Qt, Unity or [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/) when your platform strategy changes.

## When we suggest something else

If you want an open engine you can inspect, [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/) is an option. For rich visuals beyond the cockpit, a game engine may fit better. We tell you openly. See all [engines and frameworks](/work/technology-and-partners/).

## How we work

Tell us your platform, your Kanzi version and your goal. We join your project and toolchain.
