One sensor rarely tells the whole story. Fusing several makes functions like presence detection and driver monitoring more reliable.

## Where we work

In-cabin sensing and occupant understanding, with the fused output connected to the vehicle software and the HMI.

## How we work

We define the sensor set and the required confidence together with you, then build and validate the fusion on your target hardware.
