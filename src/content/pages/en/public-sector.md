Emergency work happens under stress: vibration, darkness, gloves and little time to read. We bring HMI, real-time 3D and AR from automotive cockpits to police, fire and rescue services, ambulance crews and civil protection organizations.

## Where we help

- **In the vehicle:** driver and crew displays that put dispatch data, route, vehicle status and cameras in one clear layout.
- **On scene:** the same live picture on tablets, wearables and AR glasses, hands-free where it matters, with thermal and low-light overlays.
- **In the control room:** a shared view of units, sensors, drone video and 3D maps, so everyone works from the same state.
- **Planning and training:** [digital twins](/solutions/services/digital-twins/) of buildings, campuses, tunnels and city areas for planning, drills and [XR training](/solutions/services/digital-twins/xr-ar-vr/).

## How we build

We connect vehicle, sensor and camera data into one operator view with [sensor fusion](/solutions/services/sensor-fusion/), and run it on embedded hardware with [Rocksolid](/work/technology-and-partners/rocksolid/), [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/), Qt or Android. Ramses3D is open source (MPL-2.0), so authorities can inspect it and avoid vendor lock-in.

Public projects follow their own procurement rules. Contact us to talk about how to proceed.
