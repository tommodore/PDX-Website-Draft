Defense and critical infrastructure need software that works under pressure, on constrained hardware, with protected data. We bring HMI, real-time 3D and digital twins from automotive cockpits, hold TISAX level 3 and ISO 9001 and handle your data accordingly.

## Where we help

- **Situational awareness:** one tactical picture that fuses sensor, vehicle and C4ISR data, tailored to role and mission phase, for mounted and dismounted crews. Multi-layer maps and terrain include land, air, sea and cyber data (C4ISR, BMS).
- **AR and head-up displays:** overlays on vehicle displays, HUDs and helmet-mounted displays, anchored to the scene. Latency compensation and [sensor fusion](/solutions/services/sensor-fusion/) keep them stable during fast vehicle and head movement, also when satellite positioning is degraded.
- **Operator interfaces:** glove-friendly, role-based layouts and alerts that earn attention, for rugged screens, long shifts, low light and direct sunlight. One view spans displays, platforms and unmanned systems, with fast manual override. [AI + ML](/solutions/services/ai-and-ml/) recommendations show sources and confidence, and the operator can overrule.
- **Digital twins:** your CAD data goes through our [CAD-to-real-time pipeline](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/cad-to-realtime-pipeline/) into [digital twins](/solutions/services/digital-twins/) of vehicles and systems, also used to prototype interfaces before hardware exists.
- **Training and maintenance:** guidance in [XR](/solutions/services/digital-twins/xr-ar-vr/), AR or on a tablet, with trainer screens that reuse the real operator UI.
- **Harsh, constrained platforms:** graphics tuned for tight compute, power and thermal limits and for vibration, on embedded Linux, QNX or Android, with [edge AI](/solutions/services/edge-ai/) on the device.
- **Integration, test and validation:** we integrate into your toolchains and middleware and test on target hardware, with automated regression and hardware-in-the-loop, from prototype to support for your qualification process.

Natural extensions: remote operation of unmanned ground vehicles, bridge overlays for naval platforms and AR-guided field maintenance. Emergency services have similar needs, see [Public Sector](/industries/public-sector/).

## Engine and program

We work with the engine that fits your program: Unreal, Unity, Ramses3D, DiSTI, Kanzi, Qt and others. [Rocksolid](/work/technology-and-partners/rocksolid/) by Basemark is the spatial AI and AR platform we build stabilized overlays and fused sensor views on. [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/) is the open-source embedded 3D engine we co-develop, proven in production automotive programs. Its source is public (MPL-2.0), so security teams can review and build it, and you can maintain it in-house, free of vendor lock-in. We add specialist capacity within existing programs, integrate with your toolchains and middleware, and agree conditions before discussing confidential details.
