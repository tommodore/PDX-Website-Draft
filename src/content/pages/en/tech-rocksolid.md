Rocksolid by Basemark is a development platform and runtime for production AR and HMI. It handles positioning, timing and stabilization so AR stays precise; we build the applications on top.

## Where Rocksolid fits

- AR head-up displays and navigation overlays in vehicles
- Unified HMI where AR content and interface share one platform
- AR beyond the windshield, such as glasses and wearables for field use
- Defense and mission-critical AR and HMI

We combine [HMI](/solutions/services/hmi/) and [3D](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/) skills, connect vehicle data and sensors to the AR scene (see [sensor fusion](/solutions/services/sensor-fusion/)), and support calibration and verification on target hardware.

For interfaces without AR, Qt, Kanzi or [Android](/solutions/services/automotive-android-aaos/) are often simpler. See all [engines and frameworks](/work/technology-and-partners/).
