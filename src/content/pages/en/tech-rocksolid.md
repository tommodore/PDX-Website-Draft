Rocksolid by Basemark is a development platform and runtime for production AR and HMI applications. It handles positioning, timing and stabilization of content, so AR stays precise. We build the applications on top.

## Where Rocksolid fits

- **AR head-up displays and navigation overlays** in vehicles.
- **Unified HMI** where AR content and interface share one platform.
- **AR beyond the windshield**, such as glasses and wearable displays for field use.
- **Defense and mission-critical AR and HMI** where reliability matters.

## What we bring

- **Application development.** AR content, interaction and interface design, with [HMI](/solutions/services/hmi/) and [3D](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/) skills under one roof.
- **Integration.** Vehicle data and sensors connected to the AR scene, see [sensor fusion](/solutions/services/sensor-fusion/).
- **Calibration and testing.** Support for calibrating and verifying AR on target hardware.
- **Performance.** Low latency and stable frame rates on embedded hardware.

## When we suggest something else

For interfaces without an AR component, Qt, Kanzi or [Android](/solutions/services/automotive-android-aaos/) are often the simpler choice. We tell you openly. See all [engines and frameworks](/work/technology-and-partners/).

## How we work

Tell us your use case, the display or glasses and the target hardware. We build a focused prototype and take it from there.
