DiSTI's GL Studio and VE Studio are established tools for embedded HMI and for simulation-based training. We work with them when your program or customer uses them, and we add the 3D, performance and integration skills around them.

## Where DiSTI fits

- **HMI for embedded and rugged displays**, in vehicles and cockpits.
- **Virtual maintenance and operator trainers** built from existing engineering data.
- **Training and simulation content** that is reused across desktop, VR and AR.
- **Programs where the customer or prime has standardized** on the DiSTI toolchain.

## What we bring

- **3D content from CAD.** Optimized assets and variants through our [CAD-to-real-time pipeline](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/cad-to-realtime-pipeline/).
- **Performance.** Profiling and tuning on realistic hardware.
- **Integration.** DiSTI-based components connected to your data, sensors and other tools such as Unity or Unreal.
- **Specialist capacity** inside your program, with agreed responsibilities and confidentiality.

Where a program requires safety certification, we agree scope and responsibilities up front.

## When we suggest something else

If you want an open engine you can inspect, [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/) is an option. For other use cases, Unity, Unreal, Qt or Kanzi may fit better. We tell you openly. See [defense](/industries/defense-and-critical-infrastructure/) and all [engines and frameworks](/work/technology-and-partners/).

## How we work

Tell us your program, the tools in use and your goal. We join your toolchain.
