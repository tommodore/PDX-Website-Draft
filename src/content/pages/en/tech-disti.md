DiSTI's GL Studio and VE Studio are established tools for embedded HMI and simulation-based training. We use them when your program or customer does.

## Where DiSTI fits

- HMI for embedded and rugged displays in vehicles and cockpits
- Virtual maintenance and operator trainers from engineering data
- Training content reused across desktop, VR and AR
- Programs where the customer or prime has standardized on DiSTI

Assets come through our [CAD-to-real-time pipeline](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/cad-to-realtime-pipeline/) and DiSTI components connect to your data, sensors, Unity or Unreal. Where safety certification is required, we agree scope and responsibilities up front.

For an open engine you can inspect, [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/) is an option; other use cases may fit Unity, Unreal, Qt or Kanzi better. See [defense](/industries/defense-and-critical-infrastructure/) and all [engines and frameworks](/work/technology-and-partners/).
