A modern vehicle is a connected device. Its apps must be fast, safe to use and easy to update.

## What we build

Native Android apps for Android Automotive OS and Android Auto, plus phone companion apps, connected to vehicle properties, system services and backends.

- **Driver distraction:** built to the guidelines, with short interactions and clear feedback.
- **Resilience:** offline states, slow networks and graceful recovery.
