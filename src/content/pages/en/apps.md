A modern vehicle is a connected device. Its apps must be fast, safe to use and easy to update.

## What we build

We develop native Android apps for Android Automotive OS and Android Auto, and companion apps for the phone. We connect them to vehicle data, user accounts and backend services.

- Apps for the infotainment system, built to driver-distraction guidelines
- Integration with vehicle properties and system services
- Companion apps that share one design system with the car
- Testing on emulators and target hardware, with clean release steps

## AI where it adds value

Assistants and personalization inside in-car and companion apps, with clear fallbacks when the network or the model is unavailable. More on [AI + ML](/solutions/services/ai-and-ml/).

## What matters

Driver distraction rules shape every screen. We design for short interactions and clear feedback.

Connectivity fails sometimes. We plan for offline states, slow networks and graceful recovery.
