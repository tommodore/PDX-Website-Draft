For product, sales, marketing and innovation teams with complex products: explain the technical value in seconds, build confidence before the product exists and scale premium visuals across variants and channels. Typical triggers: a launch, tender, trade show or executive demo 6-12 weeks away, multiplying variants, or manual, fragmented asset production.

## One visual foundation

CAD, design files, materials and requirements become one system of optimized assets, standards and automation. It feeds sales, HMI, web, XR, film and configurators from one source. Success metrics are agreed before production starts: time to first demo, cost per derivative, variant throughput, asset reuse and decision speed. Final scope depends on data quality, variants, target platforms and validation needs.
