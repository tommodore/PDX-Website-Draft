Unity is a flexible real-time engine with a large ecosystem and short iteration cycles. We use it where visual richness, cross-platform reach and speed matter.

## Where Unity fits

- **Rich 3D HMIs and demonstrators** with fast design iteration.
- **Configurators and virtual showrooms** across web, mobile and large displays.
- **XR applications** for design review, service and training.
- **Digital Twins** and simulation-style applications with live data.
- **Concept cars and trade-show experiences** that make a vision tangible.

## What we bring

- **CAD to Unity.** Clean, optimized assets with LODs, variants and materials through our [CAD-to-real-time pipeline](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/cad-to-realtime-pipeline/).
- **Performance on target hardware.** GPU, CPU and memory profiling until frame rate and latency goals are met.
- **Look and feel.** [Custom shaders](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/custom-shaders/) and lighting that match your brand.
- **Integration.** Vehicle data, [Android Automotive](/solutions/services/automotive-android-aaos/), backends and sensors.
- **Production discipline.** Build automation, testing and release support.

## When we suggest something else

For very constrained hardware or a tight footprint, we may recommend [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/), Qt or Kanzi instead. We tell you openly. See all [engines and frameworks](/work/technology-and-partners/).

## How we work

Tell us your platform, target hardware and goal. We choose the engine with you, and we work inside your existing Unity project and toolchain if you already have one.
