Unity is a flexible real-time engine with short iteration cycles. We use it where visual richness, cross-platform reach and speed matter.

## Where Unity fits

- Rich 3D HMIs and demonstrators
- Configurators and virtual showrooms for web, mobile and large displays
- XR for design review, service and training
- Digital Twins with live data
- Concept cars and trade-show experiences

Assets come through our [CAD-to-real-time pipeline](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/cad-to-realtime-pipeline/) with [custom shaders](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/custom-shaders/), profiled on target hardware.

For very constrained hardware we may recommend [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/), Qt or Kanzi instead. See all [engines and frameworks](/work/technology-and-partners/).
