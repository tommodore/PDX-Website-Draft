Open roles are listed here when available. We hire engineers, technical artists and designers for HMI, real-time 3D, digital twins and AI sensing.

## No fitting role?

Send us an open application and tell us what you want to build. Include your CV and, if you have one, a portfolio or links to your work. Write to jobs@paradoxcat.com.

## What happens next

We read every application. If it fits, you talk to the team you would work with. We keep you informed at each step. We delete your data after six months without contact, as described in our privacy policy.
