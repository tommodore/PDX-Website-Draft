Ypsos is ERP and CRM software for small and mid-size businesses. It serves a different market from our automotive work.

## Editions

Ypsos comes as a single-user version in the editions Small Business, Standard and Enterprise. Enterprise is the multi-user edition with SQL client/server technology, installed in your network or in the cloud.

## Platforms

Ypsos runs on Microsoft Windows and Apple macOS, as 32-bit and 64-bit application.

## Also included

Import and export interfaces, integrated data backup and extensive configuration options.
