Space systems are complex, expensive and hard to reach. We bring HMI, real-time 3D and digital twins from automotive cockpits to screens that stay readable during long passes and fast anomalies. We hold TISAX level 3 and ISO 9001 and handle your data accordingly.

## Where we help

- **Mission control and ground segment:** operator interfaces built around roles, mission phases and alerts that earn attention, in your framework or [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/).
- **Spacecraft twins:** your CAD data goes through our [CAD-to-real-time pipeline](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/cad-to-realtime-pipeline/) into [digital twins](/solutions/services/digital-twins/) of satellites, payloads and ground equipment, with orbit, coverage and link views fed by your telemetry.
- **Training and prototyping:** procedures on the 3D model in [XR](/solutions/services/digital-twins/xr-ar-vr/) or on a tablet, with trainer screens that reuse the real operator UI. Interfaces are tested on a twin before hardware exists.
- **Sales and data:** interactive 3D of your spacecraft or ground station for trade shows and agencies, plus anomaly highlighting and interpretation of sensor and image data, with the decision staying with the operator. See [AI + ML](/solutions/services/ai-and-ml/), [sensor fusion](/solutions/services/sensor-fusion/) and [edge AI](/solutions/services/edge-ai/).

## Engine and program

We work with the engine that fits your program: Unreal, Unity, Ramses3D, DiSTI, Rocksolid, Kanzi, Qt and others. [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/) is the open-source 3D engine we co-develop for embedded systems, efficient on constrained hardware. Its source is public (MPL-2.0), so your teams can review it and keep it in-house. See [technology and partners](/work/technology-and-partners/).

We add specialist capacity within existing programs and integrate with your toolchains, simulators and ground-system middleware. Early prototypes on realistic hardware surface integration risks. Requirements are often confidential, so we agree on conditions before discussing details.
