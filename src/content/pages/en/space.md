Space systems are complex, expensive and hard to reach. Operators and engineers need to understand them quickly, on screens that stay readable during long passes and fast anomalies. We bring what we know from automotive cockpits: HMI, real-time 3D and digital twins. We hold TISAX level 3 and ISO 9001 and handle your data accordingly.

## Where we can help

- **Mission control and ground segment.** Operator interfaces for monitoring and control, designed around roles, mission phases and alerts that earn attention. Built with the framework you already use, or with [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/).
- **Spacecraft digital twins.** We take your CAD data through our [CAD-to-real-time pipeline](/solutions/services/real-time-3d-graphics/cad-to-realtime-pipeline/) and build [digital twins](/solutions/services/digital-twins/) of satellites, payloads and ground equipment that you can inspect, explain and present.
- **Orbit and constellation visualization.** Real-time 3D views of orbits, ground tracks, coverage, links and status, fed by your telemetry and mission data.
- **AIT, assembly and operations training.** Procedures shown on the 3D model in [XR](/solutions/services/digital-twins/xr-ar-vr/) or on a tablet. Trainer and simulator screens reuse the real operator UI, so skills transfer.
- **Virtual HMI prototyping.** We prototype and test operator interfaces on a real-time twin before hardware exists, so design issues surface early.
- **Sales and communication.** Interactive 3D of your spacecraft, mission or ground station for trade shows, customers and agencies.
- **Data and AI.** Anomaly highlighting and interpretation of sensor and image data, with the decision staying with the operator. More on [AI + ML](/solutions/services/ai-and-ml/), [sensor fusion](/solutions/services/sensor-fusion/) and [edge AI](/solutions/services/edge-ai/).

## We work with your engine

We work with the engine that fits your program: Unreal, Unity, Ramses3D, DiSTI, Rocksolid, Kanzi, Qt and others. [Ramses3D](/solutions/products/ramses3d/) is the open-source 3D engine we co-develop for embedded systems. It is efficient on constrained hardware, and its source is public (MPL-2.0), so your teams can review it and keep it in-house. See [technology and partners](/work/technology-and-partners/).

## Working inside your program

We provide specialist capacity within existing programs and support your internal team where graphics, responsiveness and integration limits matter. We integrate with your existing toolchains, simulators and ground-system middleware. Early prototypes on realistic hardware surface integration risks before full development.

## How we start

Send us a sample model or a screen concept, and we show you a working result in a short call. Requirements in this field are specific and often confidential, so we agree on the conditions before we talk about details.
