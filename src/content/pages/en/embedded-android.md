Many products run Android without being a phone: terminals, machines, kiosks, medical and industrial devices. AOSP gives you the base. We turn it into a product.

## Where we work

We have developed custom launchers for AOSP and build the system apps, services and platform changes around them. The same team also covers HMI and real-time 3D when the device needs a richer interface.

## How we work

Small, senior teams. Changes stay close to upstream so updates remain affordable. Regular builds you can test on your hardware.

Tell us about your device and your Android version. We will tell you what is feasible.
