## The short version

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party trackers, maps or videos. Fonts are self-hosted. We process data only as far as needed to run the site and handle your inquiries. We process your data on the basis of the applicable law (GDPR, BDSG, TDDDG).

## Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, the host automatically processes technically necessary data in server log files:

- IP address
- Date and time of the request
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Referrer URL
- Page requested

We do not combine this data with other sources. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (secure and stable operation of the site). A transfer to the USA may take place. The provider is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## Contact form and email

If you write to us through the form or by email, we store your details including your contact data to handle your inquiry and for follow-up questions. The form is handled by [form provider], which acts as a processor on our behalf. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR. We do not pass your data on without your consent and delete it once the inquiry is closed and no retention duty applies.

The form also records campaign parameters (UTM) from the address you came from, so we can attribute inquiries to a source.

## Analytics

We currently do not use analytics. If we introduce cookie-free analytics in future, we will tell you here beforehand.

## External links

This site links to external sites, for example our LinkedIn profile. Data is only sent to that provider once you follow the link. Their privacy notices apply there.

## Job applications and recruiting

This section applies additionally to applicant data. This website itself does not collect applicant data. Data is collected once you apply by email to jobs@paradoxcat.com.

### What data we process

Only information we need for an orderly application process, such as name, contact details, education and career history, references, residence status and your interests regarding future employment. Any further details are voluntary.

### Where the data comes from

Directly from you during the application, or from third parties such as business networks like LinkedIn or Xing when we approach you proactively (active sourcing).

### What we use it for and who receives it

We use your data only to run the application process and to check whether you fit us and our open roles. We do not pass it on to other companies. Internally, only the people and departments involved in your application have access.

### How we protect your data

Your data is stored encrypted on servers in Germany or the EU. We work with service providers who vouch for data security and follow the GDPR. If you suspect your data has been misused or lost, please tell us immediately.

### Retention

If we have had no relevant contact with you for six months, your data is deleted automatically. Two weeks before that, candidates whose process is not closed receive an email where they can agree to longer storage.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You can withdraw consent at any time and object to processing based on legitimate interests. We usually respond within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Write to us at datenschutz@paradoxcat.com.

## Objection to advertising emails

We object to the use of the contact details published in the imprint for sending unsolicited advertising.
