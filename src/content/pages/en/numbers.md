## Milestones

- **2014-2016:** start of HMI software development (2014), graphics for the Audi A8 (2015), series HMI for the Karma Revero (2016).
- **2017-2018:** HMI technology evaluation for BMW, then an Android MVP as the foundation for a head unit project.
- **2020:** a speech team of 16 developers, fully staffed in under 3 months.
- **2022-2025:** start of production of the BMW Theater Screen (2022) and of BMW's first (2023) and second (2025) Android-based head units.
