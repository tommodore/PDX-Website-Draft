Numbers are only useful if they change what you experience as a customer. These do.

## Why turnover matters

Low turnover protects project knowledge. You do not pay again to train new people on your product.

## How we work

Small senior teams, clear interfaces and regular deliveries. We tell you early when something is off.

## Milestones

- **2009:** founded, and still 100 % founder-owned.
- **2014:** HMI software development begins.
- **2015:** graphics development for the Audi A8.
- **2016:** series HMI for the Karma Revero.
- **2017:** HMI technology evaluation for BMW.
- **2018:** Android MVP, the foundation for a head unit project.
- **2020:** a speech team of 16 developers, fully staffed in under 3 months.
- **2022:** start of production of the BMW Theater Screen.
- **2023:** start of production of BMW's first Android-based head unit.
- **2025:** start of production of BMW's second Android-based head unit.

See the projects in our [case studies](/work/case-studies/).

## Quality and security

We are certified to ISO 9001 and hold TISAX assessment level 3. That means audited quality processes and an audited approach to protecting your confidential data, including prototype and pre-release material. Ask us for the current certificates and scope.

## Where we are

Munich and Bucharest, working as one team.
