Software-defined vehicles need a cockpit that evolves after start of production. We take Android Automotive OS from UX design to release, with OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers on production programs.

## Beyond the base platform

- **Theming:** one design system across system apps and templated apps.
- **Virtual development:** emulators and virtual platforms before target hardware exists.
- **App ecosystem:** Android apps and your own services, under your review and release rules.
- **Updateable by design:** modular, data-driven UI for over-the-air updates and feature unlocks.
- **Simulation-driven testing:** digital twin and emulator setups with regression tests.

## AOSP and AAOS training

A hands-on workshop for embedded Linux and Android developers, integrators and testers: typically three days, remote or on site, small groups, exercises from real programs, adjustable agenda and language. Covers AOSP build, customization and product variants, system services, AIDL and HAL, Android security and SELinux, AAOS architecture, car services, Vehicle HAL and vehicle properties, and new-hardware integration.
